## Before asking

1. Check the [docs](docs/) and [FAQ](#) for your question.
2. Search existing issues/discussions.
3. Include: what you did, what you expected, what happened, and your
   environment (OS, Python version, `git rev-parse HEAD`).

## Response times

This is a community project maintained by volunteers. We aim to respond
within a week. Security issues: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
