## What makes it different

- **Privacy-first:** no-LLM mode, bring-your-own-key, and local (Ollama) — your text never has to leave your machine.
- **Evidence over hype:** research answers carry citations; advice is labeled honestly.
- **Free & student-first:** every core feature works without payment.
