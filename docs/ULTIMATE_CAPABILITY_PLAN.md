## CAREER PREP

### 1. JD-Matched ATS Scoring
1. **AI-interview pre-screen simulator** — 66% of recruiters use AI screening; score the resume the way their bots will, with a "bot's-eye view" breakdown.
2. **Missing-keyword action plan** — not just a score, but a prioritized checklist: which 5 keywords to add and exactly where (experience vs skills section).
3. **Offer-probability estimator** — blend ATS score + role competition data into a "your chances" meter per application.

### 2. AI Bullet Rewriter
1. **STAR auto-converter** — one click turns a flat bullet into Situation/Task/Action/Result format.
2. **Verb power ranking** — highlight weak verbs across the whole resume with a "resume verb diversity score."
3. **Metric finder** — AI suggests plausible metrics to quantify ("led team" → prompts: team size? timeline? outcome?).

### 3. Resume Versions
1. **Version diff with impact** — visual diff plus "which version scores higher for this JD" comparison.
2. **Auto-tailor per JD** — one click generates a JD-specific variant (reorder bullets, swap keywords).
3. **Application log linkage** — tie each version to the tracker: which version got the interview?

### 4. Cover Letter + Cold Outreach
1. **Hiring-manager finder** — suggest who to send it to (role + company → likely titles).
2. **Follow-up sequencer** — day-3 and day-7 follow-up drafts, auto-scheduled.
3. **Reply-rate optimizer** — score the outreach on personalization signals before sending.

### 5. LinkedIn Grader
1. **AI-search optimization** — LinkedIn is AI-searchable now; grade for recruiter-query match ("will you appear when they search 'python fresher bangalore'?").
2. **Content engine** — 74% of AI-skill mentions tie to real projects; generate a 4-week posting plan that demonstrates skills, not claims them.
3. **Profile-vs-JD gap** — paste a JD, get the exact profile sections to rewrite.

### 6. GitHub Integration
1. **README grader** — recruiters open the README, not the code; score and rewrite it.
2. **Contribution story builder** — turn commit history into resume bullets ("shipped X, fixed Y").
3. **Pin optimizer** — which 6 repos to pin for which role, with rationale.

### 7. Career Assessment
1. **AI-role fit track** — new 2026 roles (AI PM, GenAI engineer, AI ops) scored against the user's profile.
2. **6-month roadmap export** — assessment → concrete weekly plan pushed into roadmaps.
3. **Re-take delta** — "you've grown in X since last time" progress view.

### 8. Salary Explorer
1. **Offer evaluator** — paste an offer, get verdict: below/at/above market + negotiation script.
2. **Negotiation coach** — role-play the salary conversation with AI feedback.
3. **Fresher vs experienced bands** — separate curves; students see realistic entry numbers.

### 9. Portfolio Builder
1. **Case-study generator** — turn a project into a structured case study (problem → approach → outcome).
2. **Live demo checker** — verify linked demos/repos are alive before sharing.
3. **Recruiter-view preview** — see the portfolio exactly as a hiring manager will (30-second scan mode).
