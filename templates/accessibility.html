Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Our commitment

Study Buddy aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA. We believe career tools should work for everyone, including users of screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, and reduced-motion settings.

What we've done

  • Skip-to-content link on every page
  • Visible focus indicators (:focus-visible)
  • lang="en" declared; semantic landmarks (main, nav, footer)
  • role="alert" on flash messages for screen readers
  • prefers-reduced-motion respected site-wide
  • Decorative elements marked aria-hidden="true"

Timed assessments

If you need extra time on timed aptitude tests due to accessibility needs, contact us — we will accommodate you. No medical documentation required; just ask.

Known limitations

  • Some older interactive widgets (flashcards, kanban) are being migrated to full keyboard support.
  • PDF rendering fidelity varies for scanned documents.

Feedback

Found a barrier? Email accessibility@studybuddy — we treat these reports as bugs and fix them promptly.
